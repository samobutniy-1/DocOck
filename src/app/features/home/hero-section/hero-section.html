Welcome to DocOck's Files

Somewhere between a detective's desk and a hacker's den, secrets have been piling up for years — codes scratched onto old scrolls, messages tapped out in dots and dashes, ciphers too clever to crack with the naked eye. Pull a tentacle, pick a mystery, and see how far down the rabbit hole goes — from ciphers a child could break in an afternoon, to ones that took mathematicians decades. No promises you'll leave smarter. But you'll definitely leave a little more suspicious of every "innocent" string of letters you see again.
